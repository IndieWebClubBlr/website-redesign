IndieWebClub Bangalore is part of a growing world-wide network of meetups for people who want to take back their web experience from social media silos, own their online identities and content, or just get support with blogging.

## Who it's for

Bloggers, tinkerers, designers, developers, writers, and anyone who is curious about having a website. You do not need a tech background or any experience with building a site. You can start with a Google Doc or your local notes app.

## When and where we meet

We usually meet on the 1st and 3rd Saturdays of each month at Underline Center, Indiranagar, Bangalore. Check the [events page](/events/) for the next date and tickets.

## What happens at a meetup

People bring what they are working on, share what they have learned, ask for help, and talk about the web. There is no pressure to present. Showing up and listening is enough.

## How to join

<p>Join the <a href="/whatsapp" rel="me">WhatsApp group</a>, say hello on <a href="https://underline.center/c/indiewebclub/10" rel="me">Discourse</a>, follow us on <a href="https://indieweb.social/@blr" rel="me">Mastodon</a>, or <a href="mailto:blr@indiewebclub.org">email us</a>. You can also subscribe to the <a href="/newsletter/">weekly newsletter</a> or the <a href="/blogroll.atom">blogroll feed</a>.</p>

## Community standards

We expect everyone to follow the [Code of Conduct](/coc/). The site is lightly moderated by Ankur and Tanvi. If you find something objectionable, please [email the moderators](mailto:blr+mod@indiewebclub.org).

## Webring

Members can join the [webring](/webring/) to connect their websites and make it easier to discover each other's work.
